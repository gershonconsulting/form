1. WHAT THIS REPO IS
----------------------------------------------------------------
Product / site name : form.gershonCRM.com - conversational client onboarding
Live URL            : https://form.gershoncrm.com
What it does        : Client pastes their website URL; Claude (web_search)
                      pre-fills the onboarding sections; the client confirms
                      in a chat UI, accepts terms and signs. Each signed
                      intake becomes a row (with quote files as attachments)
                      in the "Submissions" table of the Airtable base
                      "Gershon Onboarding", plus a courtesy email to the team.
Who uses it         : New Gershon clients (external); the team receives the
                      notification.
